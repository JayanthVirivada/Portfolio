About

I graduated from Northeastern University Toronto's Master of Professional Studies program in Cloud Computing Application & Management (GPA 3.6/4.0), where coursework in cloud architecture, information security, and database management met a capstone that put me in charge of a nine-member research team.

That team spent the term investigating infrastructure- and protocol-level risk across three emerging enterprise AI protocols — MCP, A2A, and OpenClaw — for a sponsor, COHUMAIN Labs. We classified 48 threat vectors against CVSS 3.1 and MITRE ATLAS v5.6, benchmarked findings against known critical-severity CVEs, and shipped a NIST CSF 2.0-aligned mitigation playbook mapped across Finance, Healthcare, and SaaS.

Before Canada, I worked as an S&T Engineer on live, safety-critical railway signalling and telecommunications infrastructure in India — a zero-failure-tolerance environment that shaped how I approach change control, validation, and documentation today. I carried that discipline straight into cloud and security work: verify before you deploy, document what you touched, and never skip the maintenance window.

Outside formal roles, I build small end-to-end systems to keep my hands on the tools — a job-posting aggregator, a full-stack library system deployed to production, a CRUD cookbook app — because reading about deployment and actually shipping something are different skills.
